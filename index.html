Clarity before hype

The vision is ambitious.
The claims should stay honest.

A community-powered project building a partner application: people who own robotaxis connect them to the network so the vehicle can take paid rides. The token is the spark. The software is the product.
The partner who owns the robotaxi. CAB AI is the application that brings riders, Live View, records, and support. It is not designed as a company that buys the fleet.
A planned one-of-a-kind feature: a private live stream of the vehicle so the rider can confirm the unit is in order. Access is designed as a $2.99 add-on so the stream stays that customer’s — not a public feed. It includes recording and automatic save to the rider’s phone for evidence or personal security.
Two lines. CAB AI Support connects the vehicle and rider to the platform desk. Extensive Support opens a conversation directly with the owner who listed that robotaxi.
